FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I get access to the facility?

Access is granted exclusively through our online booking system. After you create an account and complete your first booking, you will receive the access code via message for your scheduled session. The access code is valid 15 minutes prior to your session and expires 15 minutes after your session.

How far in advance can I book a court?

You can book courts up to 7 days in advance. We recommend booking early to secure your preferred time slot!

How do I operate the ball machine?

Step-by-step instructions are available beside the tablet at your court. You can control the machine's feed rate, oscillation and ball speed via the tablet. Please keep the ball speed below 60 unless you aim to practise volleys. The system is designed for easy self-service.

What should I bring?

Just bring yourself and your athletic wear! We provide the tennis balls for use with our ball machines. Non-marking indoor tennis shoes are mandatory for court safety and preservation.

What if I have a problem during my session when no one is there?

For technical emergencies, message our 24/7 emergency line at 289-937-6561. For non-urgent issues or questions, email us at sales@funpst.com and we will respond within a few hours.

Are there security measures in place?

Absolutely. The facility is equipped with 24/7 video surveillance, automated lighting, and secure, keyless entry that is only active during your booked time slot, for your safety and the security of our equipment.

All bookings are final. We do not offer order cancellations, rescheduling, or refunds under normal circumstances. Once a booking is confirmed, the time slot is reserved exclusively for you.

Emergency situations: based on the situation, cancellation fees may be waived or returned. Please email us at sales@funpst.com to explain your situation. We will review each case individually and, at our sole discretion, determine if a refund or credit is appropriate.

Private/group lesson cancellations

Private lesson reschedules must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations or reschedule requests made within 24 hours of the scheduled time are non-refundable.

Group lesson reschedules are final and not subject to modification.
